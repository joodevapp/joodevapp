Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

  • No account. We never ask for your name or email address.
  • Your saved spots and settings stay on your phone.
  • Your location is only used to look up sunshine data for the spot you measure.
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you choose whether ads may be personalised.

Who we are

Balko is a mobile app made by an independent developer ("we"). For any question about this policy or your data, write to joo.dev.app@gmail.com.

Location

When you measure a spot, Balko reads your phone's GPS position while the app is open. It never uses your location in the background.

Only the coordinates (latitude and longitude) are sent:

  • to PVGIS, run by the European Commission's Joint Research Centre, to calculate the yearly and monthly solar yield;
  • to your phone's built-in map service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) to show the place name and to find your country, which sets the default electricity price and currency.

We do not keep your location on any server. If you do not allow location, Balko uses your phone's language and region settings to guess your country, and you cannot measure a spot.

What stays on your phone

Saved spots (name, coordinates, direction, tilt, results), your settings (electricity price, currency, kit price, system size, language) and the end time of an ad-free period are stored only on your phone. Delete a spot in the app, or uninstall Balko to remove everything.

When you share a result, your phone's share sheet sends it to the app you choose.

Ads

Balko is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, a full-screen ad between some calculations, and an optional video ad you can watch for 24 hours without ads.

To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google describes this in its privacy policy and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Balko asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy policy › Privacy choices (ads).
  • iPhone: iOS asks whether Balko may track you across apps. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised with it.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Electricity price data

Balko may download a small file with average electricity prices per country from this website. The request contains no personal data. Like any website, the host (GitHub) may keep standard access logs such as your IP address.

Feedback by email

If you send feedback from the app, we receive your email address, your message and the app version, system version and phone model the app adds to the subject line. We use them only to answer you and delete them when they are no longer needed.

No analytics

Balko does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

Balko is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Legal basis and your rights (GDPR)

We use your location to provide the calculation you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Personalised ads are shown only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); non-personalised ads and fraud prevention rely on our legitimate interest in funding the app (Art. 6(1)(f)). Replying to feedback emails also relies on legitimate interest.

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of the personal data we hold about you (in practice, only feedback emails) by writing to joo.dev.app@gmail.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
